Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Cortex Mail is an AI-assisted attention layer on Gmail. This policy explains what Google data we access, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Access

When you connect your Google account, Cortex Mail requests access to:

  • Gmail: We sync message metadata and content to classify, score, and display your mail. We also apply mailbox actions you initiate in the app (for example mark read/unread, star, archive, move to trash) via the Gmail API.
  • Google Calendar: We may read calendar data to surface meetings and deadline context.
  • Basic profile: Name, email address, and profile picture for your account.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Classify and prioritize email and compute Cortex Score
  • Extract deadlines and action items where possible
  • Answer natural-language questions about your recent inbox (“Brain”)
  • Send in-app notifications for high-priority items
  • Perform Gmail label/mailbox changes only when you take that action in Cortex Mail

We do not sell your email content. We do not use your Gmail data for advertising.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Gmail OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest before storage.
  • Synced email content is stored in our application database and scoped to your account.
  • API traffic uses HTTPS in production.
  • Optional AI providers (e.g. Gemini/Claude) may receive email excerpts you ask the product to analyze; those providers have their own policies.

4. Data Retention

Email data is retained while your account is active. Contact us to request account and data deletion.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google APIs: Gmail and Calendar — Google Privacy Policy.
  • Optional AI APIs: Used for classification and Brain Q&A when configured.
  • Hosting / database: Application host and Postgres provider process data under their terms to run the service.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Cortex Mail’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Your Rights

  • Revoke access anytime at Google Account Permissions.
  • Request deletion of your Cortex Mail data by contacting us.
